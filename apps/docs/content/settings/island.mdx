Carbon Island is a small panel that floats over other Mac apps. Use it to send a follow-up, start a task, dictate or answer an agent without returning to Carbon's main window.

Open Settings → **Island** to turn it on or off and change its controls. You can also right-click the Island to change its **Position**, **Layout** and **Show Messages** setting, or choose **Quit Carbon Island**.

## Open and write

By default, press and release `⌃ Control` on its own to open the panel. Press it again while the panel is open to dictate. A double tap from closed opens it listening. In Settings, **Shortcut** can use Control, Option or Command; **Open with** chooses one tap or two.

Pick a task from the panel's list or search with `⌘F`. You can also start a **New task** and pick its project, agent, model and Worktree setting. Type your prompt and press `⏎` to send. `⌘⏎` adds the text to the Island's draft without sending, so you can build a prompt in parts. `⌘S` saves a text-only task draft to Carbon's sidebar for later.

Dictation uses the model and microphone configured in [Settings → Transcription](/settings/dictation). If no model is ready, the Island tells you to pick one there.

`⌘Esc` closes the Island and discards a recording. When no field or menu needs it, `Esc` also closes the panel.

## Capture a screenshot

Press `⌘⇧2` from any app, or use the camera button, to capture into the Island's draft. You can annotate a capture before sending it with your prompt. macOS may ask for Screen Recording access.

Change **Screenshot key** in Settings → Island. Click the keys, press a new combination with `⌘`, `⌃` or `⌥`, or press Backspace to remove it. The reset button restores the default. If another app already uses it, Carbon asks you to pick another.

## Answer an agent

The Island shows pending permission requests and questions for sessions on This Mac. Pick an option, type an answer where offered, or use the numbered keys. A finished task can appear as a completion pill with an action to open it in Carbon.

One-time secret requests open in Carbon's main window instead. See [Permissions and questions](/using-carbon/permissions#one-time-secrets).

## Choose its appearance

- **Position**: **Notch** hangs it from the top of the screen; **Bottom** places it above the Dock.
- **Layout**: **Compact** puts the orb beside the input; **Expanded** puts it above.
- **Messages**: show or hide the conversation. Pinch to hide messages and spread two fingers to bring them back where the gesture is available.

## Limits

- Island is available on macOS and connects to This Mac's background server.
- Its global modifier and screenshot keys are configured in Settings → Island, separately from the main app's Shortcuts tab.
- Quitting the Island closes its panel. It does not settle your sessions.
